WorldsBestDay: 5 November is the World's Best Day

Which is the world's best day? The answer is 5 November. Every year on 5 November, people around the world celebrate the World's Best Day by doing one good thing, for themselves and for someone else. WorldsBestDay (also written worldsbestday or Worlds Best Day) is the name of the day.

Next World's Best Day 5 November

Why 5 November?

  1. It is autumn in the north and spring in the south, so half the world has golden leaves and the other half has fresh flowers.
  2. It is far from New Year's pressure and far from holiday rush. Nobody expects anything from 5 November, which makes it free to be great.
  3. Five is the number of fingers on a hand, perfect for a high five.
  4. It is the 309th day of the year: late enough to look back on what you did, early enough to still finish strong.

How to celebrate the World's Best Day

  • Do one thing you have been putting off.
  • Tell one person why their day matters to you.
  • Give five high fives, five compliments, or five minutes of help.
  • Post a photo of your best moment with #WorldsBestDay.

Questions

Which is the world's best day?

5 November is the World's Best Day. It is celebrated every year on 5 November.

When is World's Best Day?

World's Best Day falls on 5 November every year, no matter which day of the week it is.

Is World's Best Day an official holiday?

No. It is a community day anyone can join. You don't need a day off to celebrate it.

What is WorldsBestDay?

WorldsBestDay is World's Best Day, celebrated every year on 5 November. Search worldsbestday or use the hashtag #WorldsBestDay to join.

Who started World's Best Day?

World's Best Day was started in 2026 by Jeet Bhuva in Ingolstadt, Germany.
